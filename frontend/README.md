# GramBiz Advisor

Build: GramBiz AI — Hyper-Local Rural Business Intelligence Platform

Build a production-quality, responsive web application called GramBiz AI.

The product is an AI-powered business advisory platform for rural and semi-urban entrepreneurs. Its purpose is to help a first-time entrepreneur determine:

Whether a proposed business is viable in their local area.

What opportunities and threats exist locally.

How much business capital they can realistically structure.

Which government financing scheme they qualify for.

Their expected loan amount, EMI, repayment schedule, and working-capital requirements.

The application should feel like a combination of:

AI business consultant

Local market research assistant

Government scheme eligibility calculator

Financial planning dashboard

The target user may have very limited financial/business knowledge, so the UX must be extremely simple, visual, multilingual-friendly, and mobile-first.

40. MOST IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

The product should answer the user's fundamental question:

"Given where I live, how much money I have, and the business I want to start — is this a sensible business for me, how should I structure the funding, and what will repayment look like?"

Every screen should contribute to answering that question.

Build the product as a real working prototype, not merely a visual mockup.

Prioritize a polished, convincing end-to-end experience suitable for a national-level hackathon demo.
